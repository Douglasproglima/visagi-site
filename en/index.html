FAQ

Before you download.

Does the app show me what I'd look like with the cut?

No. Visagi doesn't render your face with a different haircut. It analyzes your three photos and describes in writing what to ask your barber for in each track.

My beard has less density on the cheeks. Does it still work?

Yes. The analysis accounts for beard density and hairline pattern. The Impact track only shows up when what you have today can actually hold the cut.

Are my photos stored?

No. Photos stay only in the capture session's memory and are used only to generate the analysis. See the privacy section for detail.

What if a photo comes out bad?

The camera warns you when the light is too low. In the review step you can retake any angle. If a photo is hard to read, the result flags that confidence is reduced.
